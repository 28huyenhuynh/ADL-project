Tarot card images in this folder

Artwork:  the Rider-Waite-Smith tarot deck, illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith
          under the direction of A. E. Waite, first published by Rider & Co., London, 1909.
          The original artwork is in the public domain.
Digital file: "78 Tarot Cards" on Figma Community
          by Ashley L.
          https://www.figma.com/community/file/1276447799586098149/78-tarot-cards
Licence:  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
          https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes:  exported from Figma (via the Figma MCP server and scripts/export_figma_cards.py),
          converted to WebP and resized. No other changes were made to the artwork.

The same credit is shown in the web interface under the card and in the project
README (TAROT_AUTHOR in rag/config.py).
