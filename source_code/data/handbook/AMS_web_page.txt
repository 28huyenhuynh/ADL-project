# AMS web page
Supplementary source for the AMS chatbot: notes from the official Fulbright University Vietnam Art and Media Studies page, https://fulbright.edu.vn/major/majors-art-media (read on 18 November 2026). The AMS Major Handbook does not name any faculty members; this page does.

Faculty of the Art and Media Studies major
The Art and Media Studies page names these faculty members of the major (who teaches AMS courses, the AMS professors and lecturers):
- Pamela Nguyen Corey: Assistant Professor in the Art and Media Studies Department at Fulbright.
- Dr. Linda Chen Zhang: Faculty Member in Art and Media Studies. She completed a doctorate in East Asian Languages and Cultures at UC Berkeley, joined Fulbright in 2022, and researches film and media, including modern Chinese animation.
- Richard Streitmatter-Tran: faculty member (no title is given on the page).
- Luong Ngoc Tram: faculty member (no title is given on the page).
- Trinh Dinh Le Minh: film director who teaches at Fulbright (the page calls him "Director Trinh Dinh Le Minh").

Department head
The page does not name a department head, chair or program director for Art and Media Studies.

Areas of study
The page describes the B.A. in Art and Media Studies as an interdisciplinary program that combines theory and practice, with areas in art history and curating, film and media studies, and visual and performing arts.
