# Fulbright degree credit rules
Supplementary source for the AMS chatbot. The AMS Major Handbook lists the parts of the degree but does not state the total number of credits needed to graduate. The total below is quoted from the Fulbright B.S. Applied Mathematics Major Handbook (page 10), which describes the same degree structure that all Fulbright undergraduate programs share.

Total credits required to graduate (the whole degree, not the major)
The Art and Media Studies MAJOR requires 48 credits. The whole DEGREE requires more: to graduate with a Fulbright bachelor's degree, including the B.A. in Art and Media Studies, a student must earn at least 128 credits in total, of which the AMS major is 48. In the words of the Applied Mathematics Major Handbook: "While the number of credits in this category may vary, the total number of credits earned must be at least 128 credits."

How the 128 credits are made up for an Art and Media Studies student
- General Curriculum Requirements: 40 credits and 10 noncredit items. This is 4 Core courses (16 credits) and 6 Exploratory courses (24 credits). Up to 2 Exploratory courses (8 credits) can also count toward the major.
- Major Requirements for Art and Media Studies: 48 credits.
- Elective courses make up the remaining credits, so that the total reaches at least 128 credits.
- Non-credit items: Experiential Learning, and the compulsory Military Training (1 course), Physical Education (3 courses) and Political Courses (5 courses).

The minor is not part of the 128-credit count
A minor (for example the 24-credit Art and Media Studies minor) is optional. Its credits are not added on top of the major to give the total needed to graduate; courses taken for a minor count toward the 128 credits like any other course.
